# The visual system, as read from the book

This is what the site was built from. If something on the site looks wrong, check it against this first.

## Sources inspected
Front and back cover (6×9 and 170×240 builds), the 278-page interior PDF (title page, map of the book on p.11, case-evidence pages, chapter openers with the loop bar, body pages, the Five Readings closing dashboard, the Scientific Heartbeat), the state-line artwork, the pulse mark, the social set, the press pack copy, the manuscript dump.

## Typography
- **Cover word:** Bebas Neue Bold, red, enormous. Used once on the site, in the hero.
- **Secondary cover type:** DIN Condensed / DIN Alternate Bold, tracked wide, uppercase. On the site: eyebrows, nav, buttons, the back-cover headline. Stack: DIN Alternate (built into Apple devices) → din-2014 (Adobe kit) → Barlow Semi Condensed.
- **Interior titles:** IvyPresto Display Regular, ~24–27pt, tight, never bold. On the site: every section title. Stack: ivypresto-display → Instrument Serif.
- **Body:** Source Sans Pro Regular 10/13. On the site: Source Sans 3, 17–20px, leading 1.55–1.62, measure 60–66 characters.
- **Instrument text:** IBM Plex Mono, small, tracked +20. On the site: times, labels, the back-cover table, annotations, the audit line.
- **Running heads:** Avenir Next UltraLight tracked. On the site: Source Sans 300, tracked 0.22em, quiet grey.

## Colour
- Cover red `#B5291C`, cream `#F7F3EC`, ink `#111`.
- Interior page `#FFFDF9`.
- The three loops, sampled from the state line: Safety `#C75043`, Time `#4B839A`, Attention `#988349`, with their pale tints.
- Rules are ink at 14% opacity. No greys other than the running-head grey `#8A857E`.

## Graphic devices carried over
- The red band with reversed tracked caps (cover foot, back-cover foot) → the band after the proposition and the footer.
- The bracket corners around a short line (cover) → "Check the signal".
- The pulse mark → nav mark, hero rule, audio player, footer.
- The struck-through sentence with a mono instrument annotation (cover ground) → the signature interaction.
- The vertical loop bar beside chapter openers → the margin bar in the signature interaction.
- Eyebrow + short red rule (back cover, p.11 map) → section openers.
- The INPUT / FIRST READING / POSSIBLE STATE table → the proposition section, verbatim.

## Spacing character
Wide margins, one column of text, deep space above chapter titles, very little decoration. Sections on the site sit in a 4/8 grid with the title in the narrow column, the way the interior sets a label above and to the left of the content.

## What stays invariant on the web
Cream ground, red used for emphasis only, serif titles never bold, mono for anything measured, tracked DIN caps for anything navigational, no gradients, no shadows except the one under the cover, no icons beyond play/pause and the pulse mark.

## The signature interaction: a day, read twice
Chapter Zero is the book's method in miniature: a morning that feels like a career problem and is a sleep problem. The cover already prints the device (a sentence struck through, an instrument reading beside it in mono). The site does the same thing with scroll: each moment of Katrin's morning appears as she read it, and as the visitor reaches it the sentence is struck through and the instrument readings appear, while the margin bar fills with the colour of the loop that was running. Nothing to learn, no hijacking, and with reduced motion it renders fully read.
