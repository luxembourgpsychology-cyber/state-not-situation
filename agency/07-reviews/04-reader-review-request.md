# 04 Asking readers for honest reviews

Prepared 16 October 2026 by the Reviews, Rights and Endorsements desk. Wording for Ivana to approve and use. She writes as "I".

The rules this wording stays inside, as read on 16 October 2026 in search extracts of the platforms' own pages (the pages themselves could not be loaded from this machine; URLs given so they can be checked):

Amazon Community Guidelines (https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF) and the Customer Reviews help page (https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G3UA5WC5S5UUKB5G): no review in exchange for anything of value, including a free or discounted product; incentivised reviews are prohibited outside Amazon's own Vine programme; reviews by friends, relatives, employers, business associates or anyone perceived to have a close personal relationship with the author are removed.

Goodreads Author Guidelines (https://www.goodreads.com/author/guidelines): an author or publisher may give free or discounted copies but may not require a review in exchange or attempt to influence it; paid and incentivised reviews are prohibited; authors should not message members who have shelved the book, which Goodreads treats as spam.

Professional ethics: Ivana does not ask clients, past or present. Nothing here is to be used in the practice, on its website, or in any clinical setting.

## What not to do

No free or discounted copies in exchange for a review. A review copy may be given to a reviewer or a reader with no condition attached, and the reader must say in the review that they received a free copy if they do write one. Do not ask for the review as a condition.

No reviews from family, friends, colleagues at the practice, business associates or anyone who could be seen as close to you. Amazon removes them and may flag the book. If a friend asks how to help, ask them to tell someone else about the book, not to review it.

No asking for a positive rating, a five star rating or a "good" review. Ask for an honest one, in those words, every time.

No replying to reviews, positive or negative, on either platform. Not to correct a fact, not to thank. A reply from the author changes the review page into a conversation and tends to attract more of what it answers. If a review contains a factual error that matters, note it for a later printing and leave the review alone.

No messaging Goodreads members who have shelved or rated the book. Goodreads treats this as spam.

No review swaps with other authors, and no paid review farms or "review services" that promise a number of reviews. Paid editorial reviews from a named publication that labels them as paid (Kirkus Indie, Clarion, BookLife Reviews) are a different thing and are covered in 01-review-outlets.md; they do not go on Amazon as customer reviews.

No asking clients, former clients or anyone in the practice's care, ever, in any form.

No reading reviews aloud or quoting customer reviews on the website without the reviewer's written permission; Amazon and Goodreads reviews belong to their writers.
