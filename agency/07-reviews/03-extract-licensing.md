# 03 Extract licensing: offer of first serial or extract rights

Prepared 16 October 2026 by the Reviews, Rights and Endorsements desk. Draft for Ivana to approve. She signs as "I".

Rights position: Budisin Publishing is the publisher (page iv) and © 2026 Budisin Publishing. Ivana is the publisher as well as the author, so she holds all rights and can decide alone, without a publisher's permissions department. The press page already says "Extract licensing, interviews and events by arrangement."

Two pieces are offered. The first is the opening, pages 1 to 3 (the flight of 16 July 1999), about 750 words, which stands alone and is already public at statenotsituation.com/en/read and as a PDF at https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-extract-the-opening.pdf. The second is page 4, "The same day, five readings", five timed lines and a closing sentence, which is very short and could run as a stand alone piece or as a sidebar. Because the opening is already free to read on the site, "first serial" here means first appearance in a publication, not first appearance anywhere; the letter says so.

Subject: An extract from State. Not Situation., offered for publication

Dear [NAME, Ivana to supply],

I am the author and publisher of State. Not Situation., published on 15 October 2026 by Budisin Publishing, Luxembourg. I hold all rights in the book, so I can agree terms with you directly.

I would like to offer [PUBLICATION] an extract.

The first option is the opening, pages 1 to 3, about 750 words. It tells the story of a small plane that took off from New Jersey on the evening of 16 July 1999 and ends with these lines: "You operate a body that produces signals and your mind often treats those signals as truth." and "But the body's confident weather report about the world is only a draft." It is attached as a PDF. I should say plainly that these pages can also be read free on the book's website, so what I am offering is first publication in a periodical, not first appearance anywhere.

The second option is page 4, which is very short: five readings of one day, each a time of day and one sentence, closing with "The body speaks first. The mind explains second." It would sit on its own or beside a review.

Terms: [TERMS: free for credit and link, or fee, Ivana to decide]. The text is to run unaltered, with any cuts agreed with me in writing first. The credit line to appear with the extract is:

Online, the site name should link to https://statenotsituation.com. Non exclusive, one publication in print and on your own site, in English. Translation would need a separate agreement.

I am a clinical psychologist living and working in Luxembourg; this is my first book. The foreword is by Dr Kristina Herber. I would be glad to send a print or digital review copy as well.

With thanks,
Ivana Budišin
ivana@luxembourgpsychology.com

Notes for Ivana: decide the terms before sending. Free for credit and link is the usual trade for a first book with no agent; a fee is reasonable for a national newspaper or a paying magazine. Decide whether the extract may be offered to more than one publication at once; if so, say "non exclusive" as drafted, and if not, strike it and offer one at a time. Check the page 4 wording against the v50 interior before any PDF goes out. Strongest reason not to send: the opening is already free on the site, so an editor may see little in it for them; page 4 may be the stronger offer for that reason.

No extract is to be described by the publication as anything the book does not say about itself; if the publication writes its own introduction, Ivana asks to see it before publication where that is possible. The foreword is not offered: Dr Herber's words beyond the two sentences already on the site are not cleared.
