## Item 5.2 Recommendation: three worth the fee, and the reason against each

Channel: internal note
To whom: Ivana
Suggested send date: decision by 30 November 2026
What the recipient is asked to do: decide
Attachments or links: as above
Approval status: awaiting Ivana

First, Foreword INDIES, 119 dollars, by 15 January 2027. Foreword is the one of these that also runs a reviewing magazine read by librarians and booksellers, so a finalist place is seen by people who buy books for others. The reason not to enter: the fee is the highest of the three for a single category, the field is large, and Foreword's own rules exclude AI generated content, so Ivana must be able to affirm that none was used anywhere in the book.

Second, Next Generation Indie Book Awards, 80 dollars, by 12 February 2027. The cheapest first category, open to international entrants, and the one with a cash prize for nonfiction. The reason not to enter: print copies must be shipped to the United States at the entrant's cost and risk of customs delay, and the award carries little weight outside the self publishing world.

Third, IPPY Awards, 89 dollars if paid by 16 December 2026. Long established, accepts ebook entry so no shipping, results by June 2027. The reason not to enter: the fee rises to 99 after 16 December, the categories are many and a medal is shared with up to six books, so a bronze may say less than it appears to.

Not recommended: Kirkus Indie Awards, because the entry fee sits on top of a paid review; Nautilus, until the cycle year and a fitting category are confirmed; Eric Hoffer, until the 2027 fee is read on the page; BPS Book Award, not eligible.

Combined cost of the three recommended entries, one category each: 288 dollars plus one print copy and postage for Next Generation. Whether that is worth it depends on one thing Ivana must weigh: an award seal on a book that otherwise refuses to say anything about itself it cannot show may sit oddly with the design. A finalist line in the press kit is the lightest way to use any result, and nothing need go on the cover.
