This is a paid service, not an editorial pitch. As read in search extracts of Kirkus's own pages on 16 October 2026: a Traditional Review is listed from 450 US dollars (one Kirkus page shows 425), an Expanded Review from 599 (or 575), with extra charges for faster turnaround. Standard turnaround is given as seven to nine weeks. Kirkus does not promise a positive review, and the author may keep the review private. If the review is kept private, no part of it may be quoted. If it is published on kirkus.com, excerpts must be attributed to "Kirkus Reviews", words may not be added, omissions must be marked with ellipses, and online quotation must link back to kirkusreviews.com. The Kirkus Indie Awards cost a further 199 dollars per category and require a published Kirkus Indie review.

No cover letter is needed; the order form carries the book. If a short description is requested, use the back cover text above, word for word.

Notes for Ivana: a paid review is legitimate and disclosed as such on Kirkus, but some readers and journalists discount it, and the fee buys honesty, not praise. Strongest reason not to pay: around 450 dollars for a review that may be unfavourable or lukewarm, and that then cannot be used in part if you choose not to publish it.
