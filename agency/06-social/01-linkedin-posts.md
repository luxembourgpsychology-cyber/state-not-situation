## Post 4. Three ways to read a moment
Suggested date: Tuesday 27 October 2026, 08:30
Image: post-16x9-1920x1080.jpg
Sources: page 7; page 9

The chapters that follow are organised around three systems that shape how your body’s signals get weighted before your mind builds a story.

Time. What came before this moment? Sleep, meals, caffeine, recovery, the hour.

Attention. What keeps pulling me back? A message, a thought, a cue, an unfinished task.

Safety. What seems at stake? What have I observed, what am I assuming, and does anything need action?

They are not brain regions or neural pathways. They are a sorting tool, a way to ask three questions when everything feels wrong at once.

Pages 7 and 9 of State. Not Situation.

https://statenotsituation.com

Three evidence markers appear in the book. My own descriptions of them:

High. Replicated or robust evidence.

Medium. Suggestive evidence with meaningful uncertainty.

Low. Plausible hypothesis or emerging evidence.

These markers exist because the book’s own risk is the same risk it describes.
