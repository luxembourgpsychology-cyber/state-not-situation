## A note on the review request

Amazon's Community Guidelines allow an author to ask readers for a review. They do not allow offering anything in return, asking for a positive review, or asking only people expected to be favourable. The sentence used below asks for an honest review, once read, and offers nothing. WARNING, read before anything else. A clinical practice's client list, past or present, was collected for the purpose of providing care.
