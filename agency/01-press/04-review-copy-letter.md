# Item 04. Review copy cover letter and reply template

Item: 04, Review copy cover letter (print and digital) and reply template for incoming requests
Channel: Printed letter inside a posted review copy; the same text as the body of an email carrying a digital copy; short email reply to requests
To whom: Any journalist, reviewer or editor who receives or requests a review copy. No single recipient.
Suggested send date: With each copy, from Monday 19 October 2026
What the recipient is asked to do: Read the book; consider a review, an interview or an extract
Attachments or links: Press kit https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip ; extract https://statenotsituation.com/en/read ; for digital copies, [TO CONFIRM: the file or link Ivana will send as the digital review copy, PDF of the interior or a Kindle gift]
Approval status: awaiting Ivana

## 04.1 Cover letter, print copy

Luxembourg, [date]

Dear [name, as they signed their request, or "Reviewer" if the copy is unsolicited],

Thank you for your interest in State. Not Situation. A review copy is enclosed.

The book was published on 15 October 2026 by Budisin Publishing, Luxembourg. It is my first. I am a clinical psychologist living and working here.

Its question is on the back cover: "You may not be reacting to the world. You may be reacting to your state." The opening, pages 1 to 3, follows the flight of John F. Kennedy Jr. on the evening of 16 July 1999. Page 20 then brings the same error to "kitchen scale. The version that happens every Tuesday." The chapters are organised around three questions, Time, Attention and Safety, set out on page 9, and each chapter carries an evidence marker, High, Medium or Low, with the work that limits it listed beside the work that supports it in the closing section, The Scientific Heartbeat.

The book offers questions, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional support. I would rather a review held it to that than to anything larger.

I am glad to answer questions by email, to give an interview, or to arrange licensing of the opening as an extract. Cover files, an author photograph and the extract are in the press kit at https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip. Publication details are on the page that follows.

With kind regards,

## Notes for Ivana

Check before sending: the price; what the digital review copy actually is (a PDF of the interior, a Kindle gift, or the first sixteen pages followed by a print copy), since the fact sheet records no digital review file; and whether a postal address for returns should appear on the printed letter. The sentence "The book offers questions, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional support" is adapted from the Research page; approve it or replace it with the page's exact wording. Decide whether an unsolicited copy goes with this letter or with the shorter press release only.

Strongest reason not to send: a cover letter that re-states the pitch can read as pressure. If the journalist has already read a pitch, send the copy with the publication details sheet alone.
