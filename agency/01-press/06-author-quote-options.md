These are drafts in her voice for her to pick, edit or replace. Once she has signed one, it is her own words (category 2). Each is built from printed lines so that it says nothing the book does not say. The test for each: could a reader who has never heard of the book understand it in one hearing, and does it make them want the second sentence.

Option A, the kitchen scale.
"Your body speaks first and your mind explains second. Most of the time we only hear the explanation, and we call it the truth. This book is about the few seconds in between, at kitchen scale: a message, a silence, a look."
Built on page 3 and page 20.

Option B, the misread message.
"A short message feels cold. Before you decide what it means, it is worth asking what came before it: sleep, meals, caffeine, the hour. The book is about that question, and about when the situation really does need action."
Built on the back cover and page 9.

Option C, the three checks.
"Check your jaw. Check your breath. Check your shoulders. What you find is a reading, not a verdict. The book is about learning to tell the two apart."
Built on page 7. The last sentence is new and is hers to accept.
