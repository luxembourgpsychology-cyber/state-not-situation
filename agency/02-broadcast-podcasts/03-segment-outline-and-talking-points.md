## Part A. Segment outline for the producer, 8 to 12 minutes

Guest: Ivana Budišin, clinical psychologist, Luxembourg. Author of State. Not Situation. (Budisin Publishing, 15 October 2026, 304 pages, ISBN 978-2-87996-258-0). First book.

What the book is, in the author's words: "It is less interested in teaching you to trust your instincts than in showing you what, exactly, you are trusting."

What it is not: a treatment, a mental health guide, or a diagnosis. The book says it "offers questions, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional support."

| Minute | Beat | What happens on air |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 1:00 | Introduction | Presenter introduces the guest as a clinical psychologist in Luxembourg and the author of State. Not Situation., published 15 October. Reads the subtitle: "Why your body decides what a moment means before you do". |

### The evidence and the limits

Your descriptions of the markers: High, "Replicated or robust evidence." Medium, "Suggestive evidence with meaningful uncertainty." Low, "Plausible hypothesis or emerging evidence."

Page 242: "Each chapter has three parts here: a short note on where the thinking came from, a list of the work the chapter is built on, and a list of the work that limits it, complicates it, or explains the same evidence differently."
