How the contacts were checked: direct loading of podcast websites was blocked from the working environment on 16 October 2026. Each podcast below was confirmed to exist through its own domain appearing in a domain-restricted web search, and the URL of its contact or about page is recorded. Where an address was legible in the excerpt of the podcast's own page it is recorded and marked "seen in search excerpt, page not loaded". Where the page offers a form or a link whose address could not be read, the contact is [CONTACT TO CONFIRM] with the URL to use. No host or producer is named in any pitch. One pitch per podcast is written to the show, not to a person.

Common offer in every pitch: a digital review copy, and a print copy on request. Common links: https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip, https://statenotsituation.com/en/press, https://statenotsituation.com/en/read.
