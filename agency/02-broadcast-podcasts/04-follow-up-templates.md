# 04. Follow-up templates

Prepared 16 October 2026 by the Broadcast and Podcast Booker. Two templates: a follow up one week after a pitch, and a confirmation once an interview is booked. Both are drafts for Ivana to approve. Square brackets mark the fields to fill before sending.

---

## Item 4.1: Follow up, one week after a pitch

Channel: Email, as a reply in the same thread as the original pitch
To whom: Whichever programme or podcast received a pitch from 01 or 02 and has not replied. Use the contact recorded on that pitch.
Suggested send date: Seven days after the pitch, on a weekday
What the recipient is asked to do: Reply yes or no; accept a review copy
Attachments or links: Press kit https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip; opening pages https://statenotsituation.com/en/read
Approval status: awaiting Ivana

Subject: Re: [original subject line]

Dear [programme or podcast] team,

I wrote last week to offer an interview about my first book, State. Not Situation., published on 15 October. I know how many messages you receive, so this is a short note in case mine arrived at a busy moment.

The offer stands. I can come to the studio or join remotely, and the segment can be as short as eight minutes. The part that works for sound is an exercise printed on page 7 that a listener can do live: "Check your jaw. Check your breath. Check your shoulders. What you found is a reading."

If a review copy would help you decide, I can send a digital copy today or a print copy by post. The opening pages are free to read at https://statenotsituation.com/en/read and the press kit is at https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip.

If the book is not right for the programme, a one-line no is welcome and I will not write again.

With kind regards,

Ivana Budišin
Clinical psychologist, Luxembourg
ivana@luxembourgpsychology.com
https://statenotsituation.com

Notes for Ivana: Send as a reply so the first message is below it. Send once; a second follow up is not recommended. Strongest reason not to send: if the outlet's own page says it does not take unsolicited pitches, a follow up will not help.

## Item 4.2: Confirmation once an interview is booked

Channel: Email, as a reply in the thread in which the booking was agreed
To whom: The producer or host who confirmed the booking
Suggested send date: The same day the booking is agreed
What the recipient is asked to do: Confirm format, duration, live or recorded, what to bring, and whether the book can be shown and linked
Attachments or links: Segment outline (03, Part A); press kit; author photograph and cover from the press kit
Approval status: awaiting Ivana

Subject: Re: [thread subject]. Confirming [date and time], and a few practical questions

Dear [name as signed in their reply],

Thank you. I am glad to confirm [day, date] at [time, with time zone] for [programme or podcast].

So that I prepare the right thing, may I check a few points. Will the conversation be live or recorded, and if recorded, when do you expect it to go out? How long will the segment run? Will it be in your studio, by video or by telephone, and if remote, which platform do you use? Is there anything you would like me to bring or send in advance, such as a print copy for the presenter, or the cover and author photograph? If the programme is filmed, may I show the book on camera? And in your show notes or on your website, may the book be linked to https://statenotsituation.com?

I have attached a one-page segment outline with a short exercise from page 7 that the presenter can do live with the audience, if that suits your format. It is a suggestion, not a requirement.

One request: please introduce me as a clinical psychologist, without Dr or professor, as that is accurate.

The press kit with cover, author photograph and extract is at https://statenotsituation.com/press/State-Not-Situation-press-kit.zip.

Thank you again. I look forward to it.

With kind regards,

Ivana Budišin
Clinical psychologist, Luxembourg
ivana@luxembourgpsychology.com
[mobile number, if you wish to give one]

Notes for Ivana: Fill every bracket. Attach the outline from 03 only if you are happy with it. If the booking is for television, add the cover-front-300dpi.png and author-photo-1600.jpg from the press kit as attachments rather than links. Strongest reason not to send: none; a confirmation protects both sides.
