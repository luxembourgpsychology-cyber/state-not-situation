# The box: how Ivana approves, and how the team works with her

Set up 19 October 2026 at her request, inside her Notion HQ (My day · HQ), not beside it.

## Where it is

Two views on the My day · HQ page, both on the existing Actions database.

"📬 Book · for your approval": every card With Ivana for the book project that is not finished. This is the box. Each card is one thing to approve or decide, with the full text in the card body, the reason, the strongest reason not to do it, and one line on how to answer.

"Book · waiting on others and on the team": cards held by someone else (a journalist, a host, the team), so she can see what is in motion without owning it.

## How she answers

Tick Finished when she has sent or done it. Write change, hold or drop in Notes otherwise, with a word on what to change. For the launch evening card, write the date and room in Notes. That is all. No email to the team is needed.

## How the team works

The team never sends anything to anyone. Emails are full texts in the card body, with the thread to reply in; she copies them into Gmail herself. Decisions are cards with the options laid out. Each check reads the cards first: Finished or Done means done; a Notes instruction is acted on; a date and room on the launch evening card unlocks the next cards (the Chronicle reply with the date, a note to the host, a note to the American Women's Club, the events record for the site). Then the signals in agency/00-SIGNALS.md. She is emailed only when someone has replied to her or a signal has crossed a line.

The team adds a Logbook row when it changes something, updates the book project's Next step to the top card in the box, and never touches the old Book post cards, the weekly brief, or anyone on the silent list in agency/11-reset/02-correspondence-audit.md.

## The campaign rule

A handful of crafted pieces, each a note from her to a specific person or room, in her register. The book quoted once or not at all. No mass pitches, no boosts, no fixed posting rhythm, no follow ups to people who did not answer. Nothing is drafted beyond the first set until she has said yes to the one page memo in the box.
