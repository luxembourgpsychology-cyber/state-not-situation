# 05. Wholesale and distribution: questions for Ivana before any bookseller letter is sent

Item: 05, questions to answer or check in the KDP dashboard
Channel: internal, for Ivana only
To whom: Ivana
Suggested send date: answer by Monday 19 October 2026; the bookshop letters in 01 are scheduled from 20 October and cannot go without these answers
What the recipient is asked to do: open the KDP Bookshelf, open the paperback's details, and answer each question below in writing
Attachments or links: none
Approval status: awaiting Ivana

Why this matters in one paragraph. A bookshop buys from a wholesaler, not from Amazon. The paperback was printed through Amazon KDP. KDP only passes a paperback to wholesalers when a setting called Expanded Distribution is switched on for that title, and even then the shop's own wholesaler has to carry the feed and the shop has to accept the trade terms that result. Until we know the setting and the terms, a letter that says "please order it from your wholesaler" may send a buyer to a catalogue where the book does not exist, which is a poor first impression. Every bookshop letter in 01 carries a bracketed line for this reason.

## The questions

1. Is Expanded Distribution switched on for the paperback, ISBN 978-2-87996-258-0?
Where: KDP Bookshelf, the paperback, Paperback Rights and Pricing, the Expanded Distribution box under each marketplace.
Why it matters: if it is off, no wholesaler lists the book and no shop can order it; the letters must then offer consignment copies only. If it is on, it was on from a given date and the wholesale listings follow weeks later, so the date matters too.

2. If it is on, which marketplaces have it on?
Where: the same page shows it per marketplace, including Amazon.com and the European stores.
Why it matters: a European shop's wholesaler may draw from one feed and not another. Record each marketplace with a yes or no.

3. What is the list price in each marketplace, and is it €28.90 or €28.99?
Where: the Pricing table on the same page.
Why it matters: the fact sheet carries two figures. One figure must be settled before any price is quoted to a shop or a library, and the Expanded Distribution royalty is calculated from it.

4. What royalty does KDP show per copy under Expanded Distribution?
Where: the Pricing table shows a separate royalty for Expanded Distribution next to the Amazon royalty.
Why it matters: that figure tells us what discount the wholesaler is working with, and whether the book earns anything when a shop orders it that way. If the figure is near zero or negative, consignment is the better route.

5. What does the Author Copies price come to, delivered to Luxembourg, per copy and for a box of 20?
Where: KDP Bookshelf, the paperback, Order Author Copies; put a Luxembourg address in and read the total before confirming.
Why it matters: this is the cost of every consignment copy, every review copy and every signed copy. A consignment price to a shop has to sit above it.

6. How long does an Author Copies order take to reach Luxembourg, and from which marketplace is it cheapest to order?
Where: the same ordering screen shows estimated delivery; it may differ between amazon.de and amazon.com.
Why it matters: a shop that agrees to a reading in three weeks needs stock in two. We cannot promise copies on a date without this.

7. Are there any author copies already in Luxembourg, and how many?
Where: your own shelf.
Why it matters: the first review copies and the first consignment can go out this week only if copies exist now.

8. What consignment terms will you offer a shop?
Suggested terms to accept or change: the shop pays nothing up front, sells at the list price, keeps a share of each sale [TO CONFIRM: typical consignment shares are between 30 and 40 percent; choose a figure], and settles and returns unsold copies after an agreed period such as 90 days. Put it on one page with both signatures.
Why it matters: the letters offer consignment as the fallback. The terms must exist before a shop says yes.

9. Can Budisin Publishing issue an invoice, and does it carry a VAT number?
Where: your own records.
Why it matters: a library buys only against an invoice from a supplier, and a shop on consignment settles against one. Books in Luxembourg carry a reduced VAT rate; if the publisher is not registered, say so plainly on the invoice.

10. Is the paperback showing Amazon's own offer for a Luxembourg address on Amazon.de yet?
Where: open https://www.amazon.de/dp/2879962587 from a Luxembourg address, or ask a friend to.
Why it matters: on 16 October only a third party offer at €53.73 was visible. Until this changes, no letter and no post should send a Luxembourg reader to that link, and the bookshop letters' argument that the book is hard to buy locally stays true.

11. Has the legal deposit been physically delivered to the BnL, and was the MyGuichet declaration filed?
Where: your records, or depot-legal@bnl.etat.lu.
Why it matters: page iv prints the deposit. The library letter 02.1 presumes it has happened. If it has not, that letter changes.

12. Does the KDP account hold the book under the author's name as publisher, or under Budisin Publishing as imprint?
Where: KDP Bookshelf, the paperback's details, the Publisher field.
Why it matters: the wholesaler feed, Goodreads and Google Books all read this field. It should read Budisin Publishing to match page iv.

## What the answers change

If question 1 is no: delete the sentence about the wholesaler from each letter in 01 and keep the consignment sentence. Consider switching Expanded Distribution on now; the listings appear in wholesaler catalogues some weeks later, in time for a second round of letters before December.

If question 1 is yes: replace the bracketed line in each letter with one sentence naming the wholesaler route the shop can use once the KDP royalty figure (question 4) shows the trade terms are acceptable to you, and keep the consignment sentence as a fallback.

Either way, questions 3, 5, 8 and 9 must be answered before any copy leaves your hands.

Notes for Ivana: the agency cannot see the KDP dashboard and has not guessed any of these settings. Strongest reason this item might not be worth the time: if you have already decided to supply shops only on consignment from author copies, questions 1, 2 and 4 fall away and the letters can go with the wholesaler sentence removed.
