## What was checked in every file

1. Every quotation attributed to the book, the cover, the author or Dr Herber, word for word against the fact sheet, punctuation included, apostrophe style ignored.
2. Every page number against the fact sheet.
3. Every URL against the fact sheet, character for character.
4. Nothing from the fact sheet's "must never appear" list stated as fact, and no fact stated that the fact sheet does not carry: prices outside the bracket, credentials, reviews, events, dates, sales, editions, audiobook, social handles, named contacts without a recorded source URL.
5. New descriptive prose about the book that is neither a quotation nor plain connective.
6. Style: em and en dashes (none found; the printed name "Demand–withdraw" is the one place a dash belongs, and the drafts have removed it there), exclamation marks (none), British spelling (one "percent"), bullets inside letter bodies (none), marketing adjectives (none in outgoing text).
7. The header block, and the line "Approval status: awaiting Ivana" (present on every item).

## The shortest summary

The pack is careful and most of it is exact. The one recurring error is a sentence about the book's structure, "each chapter ends with a note on the work it is built on and the work that limits it", written in eight outgoing texts including the Amazon listing; the page 242 sentence it rewrites describes a section at the back, not the close of each chapter. Two bookshop letters say the book was printed in Luxembourg; four pitches carry an unsourced aside about the book; the research page's sentence about diagnosis is attributed to the book five times; the author's one press release quotation has been altered. With those lines corrected, the pack can go to Ivana.
