## What the pattern shows

Twenty eight threads, almost all written in two bursts: fifteen on 23 September and five on 9 October. Eight received a human reply, two received an automatic acknowledgement, two bounced, sixteen are silent. The replies came where there was a named person who had already dealt with her (Laura Bannier, Geoff Thompson) or an organisation with a formal intake process (Ernster's committee, LLLC's forwarding note, AWCL's events officer, the American Book Center). Generic inboxes at media outlets, podcasts and bookshops did not answer: six international podcast pitches produced one polite decline and five silences; three Luxembourg bookshops produced one decline and two silences; both libraries sent only an automatic receipt. Of the eight replies, three were declines, one was a holding note, and the remaining four are open and resting with her: a journalist who wants the book, a publisher who will cover a launch once there is a date, a club that wants a talk early next year, and a magazine that wants French.
