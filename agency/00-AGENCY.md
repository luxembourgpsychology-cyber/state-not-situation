# The promotion agency for State. Not Situation.

Set up 16 October 2026, the day after publication. The agency is internal: nothing that goes out carries its name. Every message is from Ivana Budišin, in her own name, and nothing leaves until she has approved it.

## What the agency is for

To get a first book by a Luxembourg clinical psychologist read, reviewed, stocked and talked about, without the site, the press or the author ever saying something about the book that the book does not say about itself. The site's one rule governs the agency too. The fact sheet (00-FACT-SHEET.md) is the only source of facts; the brief (00-BRIEF-TO-AGENTS.md) is the only style guide.

## The roles

Agency Director. Owns the plan, the calendar and the approval pack. Decides what goes out first, checks every draft against the fact sheet before it reaches Ivana, and tells her what the team could not establish. Deliverables: this file, the launch plan (00-LAUNCH-PLAN.md), the approval pack (00-APPROVAL-PACK.md).

Publicist and Media Relations Lead. Press release, listings copy, pitches to the Luxembourg press and to international psychology and science outlets, the review copy letter, the interview preparation sheet. Folder 01-press.

Broadcast and Podcast Booker. Radio and television pitches in Luxembourg, podcast pitches, a producer facing segment outline, the on air talking points sheet, follow up and confirmation templates. Folder 02-broadcast-podcasts.

Trade, Bookseller and Library Relations Manager. Letters to Luxembourg bookshops and libraries, the Amazon listing text, the Goodreads and catalogue entries, and the distribution questions Ivana must answer before a bookshop can order the book. Folder 03-trade.

Events and Speaking Director. Letters to cultural venues, universities and institutes, employers and HR networks, schools and community groups, each offering one of the four approved sessions from /en/invite; the run sheet and the record a confirmed event needs to appear on /en/events. Folder 04-events.

Reader Communications and Email Lead. The one promised publication email to the MailerLite list, the MailerLite checks, the post publication welcome message, three personal announcement emails, the email signature. Folder 05-newsletter.

Social Media Editor. Twelve LinkedIn posts and ten Instagram captions, each built on one printed line and one press kit image, a six week calendar, comment reply guidelines, profile text. Folder 06-social.

Reviews, Rights and Endorsements Manager. Pitches to review outlets, the endorsement request template, the extract licensing offer, the reader review request wording, awards and lists with fees and deadlines. Folder 07-reviews.

French and German Localisation Editor. The press release and the Luxembourg pitches in French and German, to the standard in translation/STANDARD.md. Works after the publicist. Folder 08-localisation.

Fact Checker and Compliance Reader. Reads every outgoing draft against the fact sheet and the list of things that must never appear, and records every line that fails. Works last. Folder 09-compliance.

## How approval works

Each item carries a header: what it is, the channel, who receives it and where the contact was verified, the suggested send date, what the recipient is asked to do, attachments, and the line Approval status: awaiting Ivana. Under the text, notes tell her what she must check or decide and the strongest reason not to send it.

Ivana approves by replying with the item number and one of: send as is, send with these changes, hold, drop. Anything marked [TO CONFIRM], [PRICE TO CONFIRM], [CONTACT TO CONFIRM] or [DRAFT QUOTE FOR IVANA TO APPROVE OR REWRITE] is a question for her, not a gap the agency may fill.

Nothing in agency/ is published by the site. The folder is a working record in the repository; it is not deployed and it contains no code.
