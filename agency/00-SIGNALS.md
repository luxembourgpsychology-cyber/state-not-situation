# Signals: when the team writes to Ivana, and about what

Since 19 October the check reads the approval box in Notion first (agency/12-chief-of-staff/00-THE-BOX.md), then these signals.

Set 17 October 2026 at her request: not a fixed schedule, but a message when something has changed. The mechanism underneath is still a check that runs on a timer, because nothing on Amazon or a social platform pushes a notice to this session; the difference is that the check writes to her only when a signal crosses its line. A quiet day produces no email.

## What the team can see from here, and what it cannot

Can see. The site's traffic through Vercel Web Analytics: visitors, page views, by country, by referrer and by page, daily. The MailerLite list: subscribers, confirmations, opens of the publication email. Her Gmail: replies from any address the team has pitched, and new messages mentioning the book. Her Google Calendar: a booked talk or reading. Metricool analytics for the practice's Instagram and Facebook only.

Cannot see. Amazon in any form: price, seller, stock, rank, ratings, reviews. The container cannot reach amazon.de or amazon.com. The author's own Instagram @ivanalaruss and LinkedIn, which are not connected to any tool here. KDP sales.

The gap is Amazon, and Amazon is the signal that matters most. Two ways to close it. She forwards the KDP "sales dashboard" view or a screenshot of the listing when she looks, and the team reads it from Gmail. Or she turns on KDP's and Author Central's email notices where they exist and forwards them to a Gmail label called Book, which the check reads. Either way the first screenshot she sent on 17 October is the baseline: €29.77 to a Luxembourg address, 6 ratings at 5.0, author line "Ivana Budišin and 1 more".

## The lines that trigger a message

Site. Visitors in a day above three times the trailing seven day average, or any day with a referrer that is a news site, a podcast page or a social platform other than her own. The message names the referrer and what it probably means.
Site. A day with zero visitors after a day with coverage: the link in the coverage may be broken.
List. Any new subscriber, named by first name only, with the page they came from if known; any unsubscribe.
Inbox. Any reply from a pitched outlet, bookshop, library, venue or institution, within the hour the check runs, with a one line reading of what they are asking for and the draft of a reply for her to send.
Inbox. Any message from a reader about the book, with a suggested reply where one is owed and a note where none is.
Calendar. A new event that looks like a talk or reading, so the team can draft the /en/events record and a press note.
Amazon, from her forwards. A price above €29.77 to a Luxembourg address, a seller other than Amazon, "temporarily out of stock", a rating below 5.0 or a new written review, or a change to the author line. Each gets a message the same day with what to do in KDP.
Social, from the practice's accounts in Metricool. A post with engagement above three times the account's average; a comment that asks for advice or describes symptoms, which gets the reply guideline from 06-social/04.

## What a message looks like

One email, subject "Book: <the signal>", under 150 words: what changed, the number before and after, what the team proposes, and what she has to do, if anything. A phone notification goes with it only when a decision is needed the same day. No email is sent for a quiet check.

## What she must allow for this to run on its own

A recurring check needs a scheduled task, which this session's permission settings refused on 17 October. Until she adds a permission rule for creating scheduled tasks, the team re-arms a single check each time it wakes, and the check runs about twice a day on weekdays, morning and late afternoon Luxembourg time. The first is Monday 19 October, 07:50.
