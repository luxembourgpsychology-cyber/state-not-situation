# The series

One popular claim about the body and the mind per episode. The evidence, with sources on
screen. A one-sentence verdict. Then what the clinician does instead.

Read `BRIEF.md` first; it governs. `CITATIONS.md` holds every verified source; nothing
reaches a script from memory alone. `IDENTITY.md` carries the book's visual identity to
the channel. `PRODUCTION.md` sets format, length and what is on screen.

Each episode lives in `episodes/NN-slug/` with its research (`RESEARCH.md`), its script
(`SCRIPT.md`) and its on-screen cue sheet (`CUES.md`).

This folder is the series' own project, kept inside the site's repository until a
repository of its own can be created. It does not touch the site: nothing here is built,
served or deployed.
