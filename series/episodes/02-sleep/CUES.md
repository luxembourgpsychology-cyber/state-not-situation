# Episode 2. "You need eight hours, and you can catch up at the weekend." Cue sheet

For `SCRIPT.md` of 3 November 2026, the first script in the six beats, in the five columns
`PRODUCTION.md` defines. TC is left blank for the editor. LINE is the section's fixed first
sentence, or enough of it to find. SCREEN is one of `host`, `title`, `claim`, `verdict`,
`clock`, `questions`, `end`, `host + band`, or, new with this format, `D1` to `D4` for the
drawn pieces and `D1 + band` and so on when a source band sits under a drawn piece. BAND is
the source band's text in the `CITATIONS.md` form (author, year, journal, volume), empty
when there is no band, or `proposed` when the host marks the sentence as proposed, so an
empty band is a decision and not an omission. CIT is the `CITATIONS.md` number behind the
band; this episode's sources are 31 to 57, and 6 and 7 are not used.

One row per band change. A section that carries a band and a proposed sentence, or two
bands, has two rows, lettered a and b. The drawn pieces D1 to D4 are specified by the
motion designer from the stage notes in `SCRIPT.md`; the rows here say when each begins and
when its end frame is held. A drawn piece holds its end frame under the host's voice until
the next SCREEN change, so `host` after a drawn piece means the piece has left.
