# R01. Mogging, looksmaxxing and T-maxxing. The horizontal. Cue sheet

For `SCRIPT.md` of 10 November 2026, in the five columns `PRODUCTION.md` defines. TC is
left blank for the editor. LINE is the section's fixed first sentence, or enough of it to
find. SCREEN is one of `artefact`, `host`, `host + band`, `questions`, `end`, or `D1` to
`D4` for the drawn pieces, with `D1 + band` and so on when a source band sits under a
drawn piece. BAND is the source band's text in the `CITATIONS.md` form (author, year,
journal, volume), empty when there is no band, or `proposed` when the host marks the
sentence as her own reading, so an empty band is a decision and not an omission. CIT is
the `CITATIONS.md` number behind the band; this episode's sources are 58 to 90, and
thirteen of them are used: 58, 60, 61, 62, 63, 65, 66, 70, 71, 77, 79, 81 and 82.

One row per band change. A section that carries two bands, or a band and a proposed
sentence, has lettered rows. A drawn piece holds its end frame under the host's voice
until the next SCREEN change, so `host` after a drawn piece means the piece has left.

The artefact card and the four drawn pieces, one line each. All four are drawn on the
series' line figure, which has no face, no name and no features. Nothing drawn carries a
score, and no two figures are ever shown as one outranking the other.

Artefact. Cream; "mogging" as a dictionary entry in the serif, "noun, slang" in mono, the gloss "outshining someone or something", small mono "Collins Word of the Year, 3 November 2026"; the rule under it drawn as a scale of ten unnumbered ticks, one tick red.

D1. Two identical line figures seen from behind as raters, each marking one tick on a short unnumbered strip; the ticks land apart and the gap is red; end frame adds a faint crowd of strips whose ticks cluster.

D2. The figure's day as one ink line falling from a mono "08:00" to a mono "16:00"; the drop is red.

D3. Two rows of identical line figures at small tables; grey threads run where a chart would predict; one red thread joins a pair no grey thread joined.

D4. The line figure holding a sheet that reads "normal" in mono, before seven doors in a row; six open, one stays shut; "normal" is red.
