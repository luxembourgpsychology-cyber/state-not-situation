## The position

**Recognition before explanation.** The old posts open in the clinician's voice on a concept
("I describe a common cognitive mechanism"). The reader has nothing of their own to recognise, so
there is nothing to save or send, and saves and sends are what the account has none of.

Each post here opens on a moment the reader has lived, in her words from the site ("Work pressure
can keep you alert at night."), then shows the three-part reading of it (body, mind, environment),
then says who she is. The model's name comes last, if at all.

Strongest argument against: with a median reach of 7, copy may not be the constraint at all.
Distribution may be. Three good posts a week shown to seven people teach nothing.

The test, four weeks from the first post: twelve posts. Compare median reach and total saves plus
shares with the baseline (7, and 0). If median reach has not at least doubled and saves plus shares
are still 0, the constraint is distribution and the next move is not better posts; it is a
different route to people (her LinkedIn, the launch evening, colleagues who refer). Twelve posts is
a small sample: treat anything short of a clear break as noise.
