## 1. The lower pole of "the gap between fine and clinical"

SOURCE (page 14; the site quotes the two sentences in bold)

Put the tool down. **This book does not replace professional support and it does not treat complex conditions as lifestyle problems. It works in the gap between fine and clinical, the space where most people live on most days.** If your state does not shift after addressing timing, attention, and safety, or if the heaviness persists across well rested, well fed, low stress days, the framework is not sufficient.

CURRENT TRANSLATION (content/de.ts, book.readersAnchor)

„Dieses Buch ersetzt keine professionelle Hilfe, und es behandelt komplexe Erkrankungen nicht als Fragen des Lebensstils. Es arbeitet in der Lücke zwischen gesund und klinisch, in dem Raum, in dem die meisten Menschen an den meisten Tagen leben.“

ISSUE

Semantic fidelity, with a register question behind it. In English "fine" is a self-report: the word people use about themselves when they are neither in treatment nor well, and the book's own standing example of a reading mistaken for a fact. German "gesund" is a health status, something that is true of a person rather than something a person says. The pole therefore moves from what the reader claims to what is the case, and it moves in the one paragraph that tells the reader when to stop using the model and seek help. That is why this is not a wording question: a reader who would not call herself "gesund" may read herself out of the range the book says it covers.

OPTION A

„Dieses Buch ersetzt keine professionelle Hilfe, und es behandelt komplexe Erkrankungen nicht als Fragen des Lebensstils. Es arbeitet in der Lücke zwischen ‚in Ordnung‘ und klinisch, in dem Raum, in dem die meisten Menschen an den meisten Tagen leben.“

The lower pole stays a self-report and is marked as one; the upper pole stays the clinician's word and is not marked, which reproduces the English asymmetry between a word the reader uses and a word a doctor uses.

OPTION B

„Dieses Buch ersetzt keine professionelle Hilfe, und es behandelt komplexe Erkrankungen nicht als Fragen des Lebensstils. Es arbeitet in der Lücke zwischen gesund und klinisch, in dem Raum, in dem die meisten Menschen an den meisten Tagen leben.“

What the file says. It reads as German written by a German, needs no typographic device, and names the subclinical range the way German nonfiction names it; it converts the self-report into a health status.

OPTION C

„Dieses Buch ersetzt keine professionelle Hilfe, und es behandelt komplexe Erkrankungen nicht als Fragen des Lebensstils. Es arbeitet in der Lücke zwischen unauffällig und klinisch, in dem Raum, in dem die meisten Menschen an den meisten Tagen leben.“

A different conceptual choice: it puts both poles in the examiner's vocabulary. "Unauffällig" is what a German finding says when it shows nothing, so the sentence ties straight to Befund, the book's word for a reading. It is jargon where the English is plain, and it describes the reader from outside.

RECOMMENDATION

A. It costs one pair of quotation marks the English does not need and keeps the sentence's lower pole as something the reader says about herself, which is the distinction the whole book is built on; B is smoother German and quietly certifies a state the paragraph is careful not to certify.

CONFIDENCE

Medium.

## 2. SOURCE QUERY: "the other kind" has no first term on the site

SOURCE (page 12, opening; the site's second run begins at the sentence in bold)

A day with a crisis has a shape. It has an exact problem that makes demands and it gives you something to push against. You would know what to do with a crisis. You have done it before. **The day I mean is the other kind. The day where nothing went wrong and everything felt off.**

CURRENT TRANSLATION (content/de.ts, knowTheDay.runs[0], opening)

„Der Tag, den ich meine, ist von der anderen Art. Der Tag, an dem nichts schiefging und sich alles falsch anfühlte.“

ISSUE

Conceptual consistency, and it is a question about the English and the site rather than about German. The site opens its second section on a comparison whose first term, the day with a crisis, is on the printed page but not on the site. Both languages dangle equally, and both repair themselves one sentence later in apposition, so this is not a translation defect and no change has been made. It is raised because the editorial panel made exactly this repair once already: it added the page 12 passage in order to restore the antecedent that "that mistake" had been missing (EDITORIAL-POSITIONING.md, recommendation 2), and the same argument applies four sentences higher up.

OPTION A

Leave both languages as they stand. The section opens in the middle of a comparison, the next sentence supplies the missing kind by apposition, and nothing is added to the book's words. It reads as an opening gambit rather than as a gap.

OPTION B

Begin the run one sentence later, in both languages, at "The day where nothing went wrong and everything felt off." / „Der Tag, an dem nichts schiefging und sich alles falsch anfühlte.“ The comparison disappears and the section opens on the day itself. Nothing is added; one of the book's sentences is dropped.

OPTION C

Begin the run at page 12's actual first sentence, in both languages, so the comparison has its first term. English: "A day with a crisis has a shape. It has an exact problem that makes demands and it gives you something to push against. You would know what to do with a crisis. You have done it before." German: „Ein Tag mit einer Krise hat eine Form. Er hat ein genaues Problem, das etwas verlangt, und er gibt Ihnen etwas, wogegen Sie sich stemmen können. Bei einer Krise wüssten Sie, was zu tun ist. Sie haben es schon getan.“ Still page 12 word for word, so the site's rule holds; CONTENT_SOURCES.md gains one row.

RECOMMENDATION

C. It buys the comparison its first term with four short sentences that are already yours and that carry the section's best line ("You have done it before."); the cost is four more sentences of display copy at the head of a section the panel sized deliberately, so if the layout will not carry them, A stands and nothing is wrong with it.

CONFIDENCE

Medium.
